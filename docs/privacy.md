# Privacy Policy

**Guitar Tuner – Spot On** (package `me.saatvik.tuner`), by Saatvik Sharma.
Effective 14 November 2026.

## The short version

The app listens to your instrument to show its pitch, on your phone only. It records nothing,
collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere. It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting,
accounts or third-party code, and it does not request the Android permission needed to use the
internet.

## What the app uses

- **Microphone.** While the tuner is open and listening, the app reads sound from the microphone and
  measures its pitch in real time. Each fraction of a second of sound is analysed in memory and then
  discarded: audio is never saved to storage and never leaves your phone. Android shows its
  microphone indicator while the app listens; pausing the tuner or leaving the app stops it.
- **Settings you choose** (theme, reference pitch, in-tune range, needle, note names, tuning,
  vibration, keep-screen-on) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. If you use
  Android's own backup, Android may include these settings in your device backup; that backup is
  managed by your phone and your Google account, and the developer has no access to it.
- **Whether you refused microphone access** is saved in private storage that Android excludes from
  backups and device transfers, because it describes one particular phone.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of the above.

## Sharing

No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose tool for people aged 13 and over and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes

Future versions may add features that work differently, such as an account, purchases, or coaching
that uses online services. Before any such feature ships, this policy will be updated to describe
exactly what it collects and why, and the new version will be published at this address with a new
effective date.
