Tune your guitar one string at a time, in plain words.

Easy mode walks you through the strings, starting with the thick one nearest your face. It draws the strings as you see them when you hold the guitar and tells you what to do: "Tighten", "Loosen", "Almost" or "Tuned", with the reason underneath ("Much too low. Turn the peg slowly as you pluck."). It points out the usual beginner mistakes: turning the peg the wrong way, plucking a different string, or tuning a string far too high. When a string is in tune it moves on to the next one by itself; turn Auto switch off and it waits for you to tap Next.

<b>Advanced</b>
A precise dial for when you know what you're doing. The bead shows how far off you are, up to 50 cents either way, and the app picks the string you're playing. Tap a string to lock onto it; locked, a 12th-fret harmonic counts.

<b>Strum check</b>
Strum the open strings and every string is measured at once, so you can see which ones still need work. Strum a chord and it says so and asks for one string at a time. If the strings fit another tuning, such as Drop D, it offers to switch.

<b>Tunings</b>
Standard, Drop D, Half step down, Full step down, Drop C, DADGAD, Open G, Open D, Open E, Open C, 7-string guitar, 4-string bass and ukulele, plus a chromatic mode for any note.

<b>Also</b>
• Reference tone: hear the note a string should be, rich enough for a phone speaker to carry a low E
• Reference pitch from A4 = 415 to 466 Hz
• In-tune range of ±1, ±2, ±3 or ±5 cents
• Smooth or fast needle, sharps or flats
• A short vibration when a string reaches pitch
• The screen stays on while you tune (you can turn this off)
• Six colour themes, plus one that matches your wallpaper on Android 12 and later
• Works in split screen
• Works with TalkBack, Switch Access, a keyboard or a D-pad, and with larger text up to 1.3×

<b>Privacy</b>
The microphone is used only to hear your instrument. Sound is analysed on your phone as it comes in; it is never saved or sent anywhere. The app doesn't have permission to use the internet.
